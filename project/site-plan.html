Site Name

Name

LagosBites  –  lagos-bites.com

Why this name?

"Lagos" grounds the site in its location — Nigeria's largest city and food capital. "Bites" is playful and immediately communicates food. Together the name is short, memorable, and easy to search for online.

Site Purpose

LagosBites is a food discovery blog for locals and visitors in Lagos, Nigeria. The site will feature restaurant reviews, street-food guides, recipes inspired by Nigerian cuisine, and a weekly "hidden gem" spotlight. Visitors can browse by neighborhood, cuisine type, or budget to find their next great meal.

Scenarios

Questions a typical site visitor might ask:

  • Where can I find the best jollof rice in Lagos Island on a weeknight?
  • What are some affordable street-food spots near Victoria Island?
  • How do I make authentic egusi soup at home?
  • Which restaurants in Lekki are open late on Sundays?
